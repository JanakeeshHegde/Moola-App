Account Deletion & Data Erasure

In compliance with data protection principles and your consumer privacy rights, you may request permanent deletion of your MOOLA buyer or seller account.

Important Consequences of Account Deletion

  • All active login sessions and JWT authentication tokens will be permanently invalidated.
  • Your personal contact information and delivery addresses will be scrubbed and anonymized.
  • If you have pending orders or in-transit postal consignments, deletion will be scheduled upon fulfillment completion.
  • This action is irreversible. You will need to create a new profile to purchase in the future.

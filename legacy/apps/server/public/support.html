In-App Ticket System

If you have an active account on the MOOLA Mobile App, we recommend submitting a complaint ticket directly inside the app under Customer Support & Complaints.

  • Automatic Order Linking: You can link specific orders for faster investigation.
  • Unique Reference ID: Receive an official tracking ticket (e.g., TKT-20261022-ABCD).
  • Track Live Status: Monitor review progress through Submitted, Acknowledged, Under Review, and Resolved.
  • 48-Hour SLA: Our team aims to acknowledge and review all submitted tickets within 48 hours.

1. Introduction & Scope

MOOLA operates an online marketplace connecting verified sellers and makers of homemade food products (such as chips, snacks, honey, spices, pickles, and traditional delicacies) with consumers across India.

This Privacy Policy describes how MOOLA collects, uses, retains, and protects personal information when you use our mobile application and related web services. Please read this policy carefully.

Platform Scope: MOOLA facilitates discovery and ordering of shelf-stable homemade food products and regional specialties. MOOLA is not a fresh vegetable market.

2. Information We Actually Collect

We believe in data minimization and collect only the information necessary to provide marketplace services:

A. Account & Profile Information

  • Full Name: Required to identify your account and address your orders or store profile.
  • Mobile Number: Required for account authentication, order notifications, seller contact, and customer support.
  • Email Address: Optional. Collected only if you choose to provide it for transaction notifications.
  • Password Hash: Stored securely as an irreversible one-way cryptographic hash using bcrypt. Plaintext passwords are never saved.
  • Preferred Language: Stored to deliver interfaces in English or Kannada (en or kn).
  • Delivery Addresses: Name, phone number, address lines, city, state, and pincode required for dispatching physical orders.

B. Seller Store & KYC Information

  • Shop Profile: Shop name, store description, store logo, and optional WhatsApp contact number.
  • Payment Details: Seller UPI ID and manual payment instructions displayed to buyers upon order confirmation.
  • KYC Verification Documents: Optional identity/business proofs uploaded solely for administrator verification.

C. Orders, Transactions & Communication

  • Order Records: Item snapshot (name, unit, price), quantities, total amount, shipping address, and status timestamps.
  • Payment Reference (UTR): Transaction reference numbers provided by buyers to verify direct UPI or bank payments. We do not process or store credit/debit card numbers or bank netbanking credentials.
  • Shipment Tracking: Carrier names (e.g., India Post, DTDC, Blue Dart) and consignment tracking numbers entered by sellers.
  • Customer Support Complaints: Ticket subject, category, description, optional order reference, and status history.

D. Device, Notification & Technical Data

  • Expo Push Tokens: Device push tokens saved to deliver real-time order and support updates.
  • In-App Notifications: Notification history stored in MongoDB Atlas with a 90-day retention index.

3. WhatsApp Communication Notice

MOOLA allows buyers to initiate product enquiries with sellers using official WhatsApp click-to-chat links.

Important: When you tap a WhatsApp link, you leave the MOOLA application and enter WhatsApp (operated by Meta Platforms, Inc.). Any messages, media, or personal information shared over WhatsApp are governed exclusively by WhatsApp's Privacy Policy. MOOLA does not intercept, read, or store the contents of your private WhatsApp conversations on our servers.

4. How We Use Your Information

We process your data for the following lawful operational purposes:

  • Authenticating your account and maintaining session security.
  • Enabling buyers to place orders and sellers to fulfill dispatches.
  • Facilitating direct seller-buyer communication for order confirmation and payment verification.
  • Delivering push notifications and in-app alerts regarding order status and support tickets.
  • Investigating and resolving customer complaints and dispute tickets.
  • Preventing fraud, platform abuse, and unauthorized access.
  • Complying with applicable statutory tax, accounting, and consumer protection requirements.

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We disclose information only to:

  • Counterparties to a Transaction: When an order is confirmed, the buyer's name, phone, and delivery address are shared with the seller for dispatch. Similarly, the seller's store name, WhatsApp number, and payment instructions are shared with the buyer.
  • Logistics Providers: Delivery details may be affixed to parcels and entered into courier portals (such as India Post or DTDC) by the seller.
  • Infrastructure Service Providers: Secure database hosting on MongoDB Atlas and push delivery through Expo.
  • Legal Authorities: Only when strictly mandated by applicable Indian law, court order, or government authority.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active. You may request account deletion at any time directly through the app (Profile > Delete Account) or via our public Account Deletion Page.

Upon processing an account deletion request:

  • Your name, phone number, email, address, and profile credentials are deactivated and anonymized.
  • Active sessions and device push tokens are immediately invalidated.
  • Completed order records, invoice totals, and dispute histories are retained in anonymized format for statutory tax and audit compliance.

7. Security Practices

We employ industry-standard administrative and technical safeguards, including HTTPS transport encryption, bcrypt password hashing, JWT authorization, and role-based access control. No administrator or developer has access to your plaintext passwords.

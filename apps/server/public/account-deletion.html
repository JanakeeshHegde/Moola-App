Account Deletion & Data Removal

At MOOLA, you have full control over your personal data. You can delete your account at any time either directly inside the MOOLA Mobile App (via Profile > Settings > Delete Account) or by submitting the public request form below if you are currently unable to access the app.

What happens when your account is deleted:

  • Identity & Profile: Your name, phone number, email, saved addresses, and profile pictures are permanently anonymized or removed.
  • Seller Stores: If you are a seller, all your active product listings will be taken offline immediately, and your store profile closed.
  • Sessions & Tokens: All active sessions, push notification tokens, and authentication cookies are revoked.
  • Active Orders Check: Accounts with pending dispatches or in-transit orders cannot be immediately deleted until all outstanding orders are fulfilled or cancelled.
  • Statutory Retention: Past invoices, completed order summaries, and audit logs are retained in anonymized form as required by applicable tax and accounting laws in India.

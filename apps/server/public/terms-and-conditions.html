2. Description and Scope of the MOOLA Platform

MOOLA is an online technology marketplace that enables independent producers, artisanal makers, and verified sellers to list and offer shelf-stable homemade and organic food products—such as homemade chips, snacks, raw honey, spices and spice powders, pickles, and traditional delicacies—directly to consumers in India.

Notice: MOOLA is strictly an artisanal food marketplace and is not a vegetable or fresh produce marketplace. MOOLA acts as an intermediary discovery platform connecting buyers and independent sellers.

  • Submit accurate payment reference numbers (such as UPI UTR numbers) to verify payments.

6. Food-Related Information, Allergens & Shelf Life

Because MOOLA products include artisanal snacks, honey, pickles, and spices:

  • Buyers must carefully review all listed ingredients if they or members of their household have food sensitivities or allergies (e.g., nuts, gluten, mustard, sesame, dairy).
  • Sellers must clearly mention storage requirements (e.g., store in an airtight container, keep refrigerated, keep dry) and realistic shelf life.
  • Perishable or short-shelf-life items must be dispatched using express courier options.

7. Order Enquiries and Order Confirmation

MOOLA supports direct order requests in the app as well as pre-order WhatsApp enquiries. An order is considered confirmed when the seller formally accepts the order request and provides payment instructions. MOOLA reserves the right to cancel unconfirmed or fraudulent requests.

8. WhatsApp Communication and Payment Arrangements

Current Payment Scope: MOOLA does not currently provide an integrated payment gateway, escrow wallet, or automated credit card billing.

  • Payments are made directly from the buyer to the seller using direct UPI transfer, bank transfer, or cash on delivery (where mutually agreed).
  • Buyers enter transaction reference numbers (UTR) in the app to notify sellers of completed transfers. Sellers must manually verify receipt before dispatch.
  • MOOLA does not store bank account passwords, UPI PINs, or debit/credit card CVV details.
  • WhatsApp conversations take place on Meta's WhatsApp platform and are not recorded or hosted in MOOLA's database.

9. Cancellations, Refunds, Returns & Damaged Goods

  • Cancellations: A buyer may cancel an order request before the seller confirms or packs the order. Once an order is packed or dispatched with a consignment number, cancellation is subject to seller agreement.
  • Damaged or Incorrect Items: If a food parcel arrives damaged, leaking, unsealed, or materially different from what was ordered, the buyer must report the issue within 48 hours via Customer Support & Complaints, providing photos of the package and item.
  • Food Safety & Returns: For hygiene and health safety reasons, opened or consumed food packages cannot be physically returned. In verified cases of transit damage or wrong shipment, sellers are required to issue a replacement or refund directly to the buyer's UPI account.
